TITLE:
The King Who Was So Rich He Crashed an Economy

DESCRIPTION:
In 1324, Mansa Musa, ruler of the Mali Empire, traveled to Mecca and stopped in Cairo, where he spent and gave away so much gold that its value collapsed for years.
In this video, Sterling explains what happened, why it happened, and the simple economic rule behind it: inflation.
